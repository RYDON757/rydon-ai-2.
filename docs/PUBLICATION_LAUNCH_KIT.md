## Directory listing draft
**Title:** Chat IPA — AI assistant

**Short description:** Chat IPA is a browser-based AI assistant for chat, voice, document help, and web search, with a responsive interface for desktop and mobile.

**Website:** https://rydon-ai-2.onrender.com/

**Long description:** Chat IPA brings AI chat and everyday productivity tools into a browser-based interface. Depending on the service configuration and browser support, users can chat with an AI model, use speech features, work with text/PDF documents, and access web-search or creative tools. Hosted AI and creative features require a configured provider and may be subject to provider quotas or costs. Chat history is stored locally in the browser; account data is stored in the configured server-side database. Review the privacy policy before use.

**Important claims to avoid until verified:** do not promise unlimited/free AI generation, persistent cloud chat history, paid subscription availability, app-store availability, or specific privacy certifications.

## Android publication gate
- A PWA is not automatically a Play Store package. Choose and build a supported Android packaging route.
- Verify launcher icons, application ID, signing, asset links if required, privacy disclosures, data safety answers, content rating, screenshots, support contact, and account deletion.
- Test on the owner's Android device and complete required Play Console testing. A Play Console account requires age 18+ and a one-time US$25 registration fee; do not claim the app is listed until Google Play confirms publication.

## Apple App Store publication gate
- Create and sign a real iOS build with an appropriate bundle identifier, icons, privacy disclosures, screenshots, and support contact.
- Test on physical iPhone/iPad hardware and check current App Store review requirements. A web app/PWA alone is not evidence of App Store submission or approval.
- Use an owner-controlled Apple Developer account and verify current membership fees and requirements directly with Apple.

## Owner-control checklist
- Keep GitHub and hosting accounts under the owner's email and enable two-factor authentication.
- Keep model, image, search, and payment API keys in hosting environment variables, never in source code.
- Keep signing keys and store credentials private and backed up securely.
- Before accepting real users, configure durable account storage, verify account deletion and data export expectations, and provide a dedicated support contact.
- The repository is currently public. Anyone may view or fork it; change its visibility only after confirming the hosting integration and access implications.
